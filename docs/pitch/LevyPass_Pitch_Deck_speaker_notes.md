# LevyPass pitch: speaker notes

## 1. LevyPass

LevyPass, Team FET, team13. One line: pay the levy digitally, the money goes straight to the government's Access Bank account, and anyone can prove payment with one scan. The phone on the right is our officer app, live from the build.

## 2. The problem

Mama Bisi sells fabric. She pays cash every day and sometimes twice, because a second collector comes. SBM found 98% of informal businesses already pay, and over a third pay two or three groups. The money exists; it just doesn't reach the government. And from 2026 the new framework bans cash payment of taxes, so councils need a cashless rail now.

## 3. Our solution

Walk the loop: register, pay by USSD, officer scans, trader checks the officer, revenue office sees it. Both screens are real captures. Then go to the live demo.

## 4. Unique Value Proposition and Competitive Advantage

Cash and paper give no proof. Generic e-payment portals get money to the government but do nothing for the officer on the street or the trader facing a tout. LevyPass is the only column that closes the loop on the spot. The moat: legal grounding per levy, a ledger a banker can audit, and Access Bank rails.

## 5. Technology and security

The rule a banker checks first: verify before value. A webhook is a hint; we confirm server to server, then write everything in one transaction. On a timeout we never resend, we requery. In the demo we withhold, replay and forge webhooks live.

## 6. Business model and impact

Traders pay nothing extra. The council pays a small fee per successful collection, out of money it loses today. The ₦130m is arithmetic for one market, not a forecast; the real baseline gets measured in the pilot.

## 7. Bank fit and scale

For Access Bank: deposits from collection accounts, a route to traders the bank rarely reaches, and with consent, the data to lend to them. Next: a one-market pilot under an MOU with a council, then the whole council, then other cities.
